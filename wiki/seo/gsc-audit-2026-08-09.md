# GSC Audit. Trading852 v2.

Scope: 4 exports from GSC's Page Indexing report (`Chart.csv`, `Critical issues.csv`, `Metadata.csv`, `Non-critical issues.csv`), covering 2026-05-11 to 2026-08-05, cross-checked live against `trading852.com` (sitemap, redirects, robots.txt, homepage links) and the repo's `vercel.json` on 2026-08-09.

## Summary

| Issue | Pages | Cause identified | Action needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page with redirect | 2 | Confirmed: old slug `spy-ipo-fork-747` + its chart image, both 308 to the current URL | None — GSC will drop these on its own |
| Redirect error | 1 | Not identified — no URL in the aggregate export | Pull the URL from GSC UI |
| Discovered, currently not indexed | 13 | Not a technical fault (see below) | Manual indexing requests + backlink signal |
| Crawled, currently not indexed | 1 | Not identified — no URL in the aggregate export | Pull the URL from GSC UI |
| Non-critical issues | 0 | — | — |

17 not-indexed pages in the 2026-08-05 chart row = 2+1+13+1 exactly. Every not-indexed page is accounted for by the critical-issues table; nothing is happening outside these 4 buckets.

## What the exports can't answer

`Critical issues.csv` is grouped by reason, not by URL — it says "13 pages" but not which 13. The "Redirect error" and "Crawled – currently not indexed" rows can't be diagnosed further without their URLs. In the GSC UI: **Indexing → Pages → click each reason row → export the URL table** (or read it directly). Bring that back and both remaining rows become fixable instead of guessed at.

## What was checked live (2026-08-09)

- **Sitemap**: 30 URLs total (1 homepage, 26 `/analyses/*`, 4 static pages), all return `200`. No broken or redirected URL is in the current sitemap.
- **Redirects in `vercel.json`**: exactly 2, both permanent (308), both single-hop, no chain, target confirmed live (`spy-747-level` page and its image return 200). This matches the "Page with redirect: 2" row precisely — informational, not a bug. GSC keeps flagging a redirected URL for a while after a rename; it self-clears once Google stops trying to recrawl the old URL. No code change needed.
- **Internal linking**: homepage links all 26 `/analyses/*` pages in the sitemap. Not a linking gap — every page GSC could discover is reachable from the homepage in one click.
- **`robots.txt`**: `Allow: /` sitewide, sitemap declared. Not blocking anything.
- **www duplicate**: `https://www.trading852.com/` returns `200` directly instead of redirecting to the apex domain (`http://www.` does redirect to `https://www.`, but `https://www.` never redirects to `https://trading852.com`). This is a real gap, separate from the 4 GSC buckets above — it creates a second crawlable, unredirected copy of every page under `www.`. Not yet flagged by GSC only because Google hasn't chosen to crawl it as a distinct host yet.

## Reading "Discovered/Crawled, currently not indexed"

Sitemap has 26 content pages; only 18 are indexed. 8 of the 13 "discovered, not indexed" pages are unaccounted for by anything technical checked above (sitemap, robots, internal links, redirects are all clean). For a site this size with intact technical SEO, this pattern is normally Google deprioritizing crawl/index on a low-authority young domain, not a bug to fix in code. Two levers actually move this:

1. **Request indexing manually** on the highest-value pages (the ticker analyses, not the sector/thesis hubs) via GSC's URL Inspection tool. This forces a crawl outside Google's own queue.
2. **External signals** — backlinks and shares raise the domain's crawl priority. The IG carousel pipeline (`/ig-852`) already produces shareable assets per article; check whether it's firing consistently and whether the tweet/IG posts link back to the article URL, not just the homepage.

Chart.csv shows a genuine bump: not-indexed went from 4 to 17 on 2026-07-25, unaccompanied by any commit adding 13 new pages (checked `git log` 07-18 to 07-28 — only style/content edits, no new pages). Read this as Google re-scoring the whole site's indexation status around that date, not as 13 new orphan pages appearing.

## Plan, in order

1. **Pull URL-level exports for "Redirect error" and "Crawled – currently not indexed"** from the GSC UI — the only step blocked on Dany, everything else here was diagnosable from the files as given.
2. **Fix `www` → apex redirect** in `vercel.json` (add a redirect rule, or handle at the DNS/Vercel domain level) — a real technical gap, independent of the GSC export, cheap to close.
3. **Request indexing** via URL Inspection for the 12 ticker-analysis pages first (highest commercial value), then the sector hubs.
4. **No action on the 2 "page with redirect" rows** — confirmed correct, self-resolving.
5. **Re-pull the same 4 exports in ~3-4 weeks** to see whether indexed count moved and whether the www fix or manual-indexing requests changed anything, before escalating further.

## Follow-up, 2026-08-13: three causes the Aug 9 pass missed

Same 4 exports, re-read against the repo rather than only against the live site. The Aug 9 conclusion that the 13 "discovered, not indexed" pages were "not a technical fault" is **partly wrong**. Three concrete causes were found and shipped in [954929d](https://github.com/Marcvrick/trading852-v2/commit/954929d).

**1. `/drafts/*` was public.** `dist/drafts/` deploys. `https://trading852.com/drafts/0086-sun-hung-kai` and `/drafts/6160-beone` returned 200, carried no `noindex`, were absent from the sitemap, and their `CONFIG.canonical` pointed at `/analyses/{slug}` — a URL that 404s until the piece is actually published. Two unpublished analyses were crawlable with a canonical into a dead URL, and they emitted internal links to `6690-haier`, `special-situations` and `market-thesis`. `build.js` now forces `noindex, nofollow` plus a self-referencing canonical on anything under `drafts/`. They stay in `dist/` so the live host can still preview them.

**2. The 7 sector hubs are thin, and the sitemap submitted them.** Body word counts measured on `dist/`, `<main>` only:

```
250  biotech          325  technology              581  market-thesis
254  special-situations   331  electric-vehicles
297  luxury           352  consumer-discretionary
```

Against 1471 to 3976 for real articles. They carry no `CONFIG.pubDate`, so no `lastmod` either. Submitting a 250-word link list is what Google answers "Discovered - currently not indexed" to. `getAllAnalysisPages()` now filters on `pubDate`: sitemap went from 33 URLs to 25. The hubs stay crawlable through the navbar and the article cross-links, so nothing is lost from discovery. Give a hub real prose and a `pubDate` and it returns to the sitemap automatically.

**3. The www fix of Aug 9 ([757bd99](https://github.com/Marcvrick/trading852-v2/commit/757bd99)) did not cover the root.** `"source": "/:path*"` matched every subpath but not `/`. `https://www.trading852.com/` still returned 200 with the full homepage on Aug 13. An explicit `"source": "/"` rule was added ahead of it. Verified: 308 to the apex.

Also fixed: `llms.txt` advertised `/publish/articles.md` and `/wiki/index.md`, both 404. Replaced with `/sitemap.xml`, `/feed.xml`, `/scorecard`, `/about`, plus a line telling answer engines that `/drafts/*` is not citable.

### Correction to the Aug 9 internal-linking note

"Homepage links all 26 pages, not a linking gap" is right as far as it goes, but the editorial link graph is lopsided. Inbound internal links, measured:

```
market-thesis            51        0700-tencent              2  (homepage only)
consumer-discretionary   13        0027-galaxy               2
technology               13        9973-chery                2
electric-vehicles        11        gold-regime               2
```

`market-thesis` at 51 is mostly the navbar ("HSI") on all 34 pages, which is normal navigation. The other six hubs are not in any partial: those 9 to 13 links are editorial, and they land on the pages Google refuses to index while the 3500-word articles sit at 2.

### Source links, 2026-08-13 ([b962271](https://github.com/Marcvrick/trading852-v2/commit/b962271))

15 of 27 pages carried no external link at all. On inspection: 7 are hubs with nothing to cite, and 6 (`gold-regime`, `hang-seng-gdp-paradox`, `hong-kong-discount-cheap-two-ways`, `hsi-35-year-trendline`, `rate-convexity`, `spy-747-level`) have **no `sources-section` at all**, only the generic disclaimer, which names no document. Those 6 cannot be linked without knowing the underlying sources.

The remaining 2 name real documents and were linked, 9 links, same markup and same HKEXnews `titlesearch` URL shape the other 12 articles already use: `0027-galaxy` (IR deck FY2025, Annual Report 2025, Capella opening, Phase 4 capex to HKEXnews issuer 27; Macau GGR Mar-Jun 2026 and Q1 2026 GGR to the DICJ monthly stats page) and `1361-361degrees` (FY2025 Annual Report, share placement, AGM results to HKEXnews issuer 1361). Left plain: the Morgan Stanley note, the CLSA forecast, the 14-broker consensus, peer multiples, own calculations, the FinMC_3 price cache.

This does nothing for ranking. Outbound links point away from the site; they are not backlinks. What they buy is citability by answer engines, which check whether a claim traces to a primary source before quoting it. Half the site gave them nothing to verify.

**Open action, unchanged from Aug 9:** pull the URL-level export for "Redirect error" and "Crawled - currently not indexed" from the GSC UI, resubmit the 25-URL sitemap, and run URL Inspection on the 20 dated articles. Then re-pull the 4 exports around mid-September and compare.

**Still not addressed:** the 7 hubs need 300 to 400 words of real prose each before they deserve a place in the sitemap, and the 6 articles above need their sources named before they can be linked.
